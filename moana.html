the story:

In ancient Polynesia, when a terrible curse incurred by the demigod Maui reaches Moana's island, she answers the Ocean's call to seek out Maui to set things right.

Parental Guide

Is it good for children? Yes, highly recommended. Great themes of bravery and identity.

Bad scenes: Some scary moments with the lava monster Te Kā, but nothing inappropriate.
