ENCANTO

G Safe for Kids Musical

The Madrigals are an extraordinary family who live hidden in the mountains of Colombia in a charmed place called the Encanto. The magic of the Encanto has blessed every child in the family with a unique gift -- every child except Mirabel.

Parental Guide

Review: A beautiful exploration of family dynamics and the pressure to be perfect. Excellent music and visuals.

Sensitive Scenes: Some scenes show the family home cracking and crumbling, which might be stressful for very small children.
