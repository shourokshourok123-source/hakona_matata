VIOLET EVERGARDEN

13+ Emotional Anime

In the aftermath of a great war, Violet Evergarden, a young girl formerly used as a weapon, begins a new life as an Auto Memory Doll, helping people express their feelings through letters while searching for the meaning of the words "I love you."

Parental Guide

Review: A masterpiece of storytelling and animation. Very emotional and respectful. Teaches empathy and the power of words.

Sensitive Scenes: Flashbacks to war scenes, showing some blood and combat. Themes of grief and loss are central.
